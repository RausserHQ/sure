# Sure

Sure represents a household's financial position and activity. A debt's principal, total amount owed, and borrowing capacity are distinct quantities.

## Language

**Principal outstanding**:
The unpaid principal of a debt at a stated time, including any interest or fees already capitalized into principal under the account's terms.
_Avoid_: Total liability as a synonym.

**Total liability**:
The amount currently owed at a stated time: principal plus separately owed interest and fees not already included in principal. It excludes future projected interest and available credit.
_Avoid_: Principal outstanding as a synonym.

**Available credit**:
The amount a borrower may still draw under a credit facility. It is borrowing capacity, not an amount already owed.

**Observed balance**:
A value reported by a financial source or statement for a particular quantity and time. An observation can be retained even when the quantity it measures remains uncertain.

**Balance conflict**:
Unreconciled evidence asserting incompatible values for the same account, quantity, currency, and time boundary. Values for different quantities or times are not a conflict merely because their amounts differ.

**Reconstructed balance**:
A historical value derived from an accepted balance and the complete, known changes in the same quantity over an established interval.
_Avoid_: Observed balance as a synonym.

**History gap**:
An interval or point where the available evidence does not establish a value for the requested quantity.
_Avoid_: Zero balance or unchanged balance as a synonym.

**Known net-worth subtotal**:
The value of supported assets less supported liabilities at a stated time. It is incomplete when any account meant to contribute lacks a supported value.
_Avoid_: Complete net worth as a synonym.

**Valuation**:
A stated absolute value of an asset or liability at a particular time. It does not itself describe income, expense, or a transactional change.

**Other liability**:
An obligation outside the more specific debt categories. Its value may be stated directly or tracked through supported activity under the account's explicit interpretation.

**Projection**:
A value estimated from assumptions about activity, rates, timing, or other conditions. It is distinct from observed or supported reconstructed history.
